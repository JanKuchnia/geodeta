# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Private property owners, home builders, architects, structural engineers, and property developers in Wieliczka, Kraków, and the Małopolska region needing licensed surveying (geodetic) services such as development maps, building setting-out, post-construction surveys, and plot boundary divisions.

## Product Purpose
Provide an authoritative, modern, and trustworthy digital presence for HORYZONT Biuro Geodezyjne Łukasz Kowalczyk that clearly presents services, pricing transparency, legal credentials, and direct contact options to convert visitors into inquiries and phone calls.

## Brand Personality
Precise, grounded, professional, approachable, and transparent. The voice of an expert engineer who simplifies complex municipal and legal procedures for clients.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS templates with purple/indigo neon gradients and dark mode cyber-glow.
- Cluttered 2000s-era municipal engineering portals filled with jargon and wall-of-text paragraphs.
- Templated landing pages with endless nested cards, tiny unreadable badges, and all-caps body text.

## Design Principles
- **Engineering precision**: Clean layouts, crisp geometric type hierarchy, and disciplined spacing reflecting exact surveying standards.
- **Natural, regional grounding**: Earthy, credible forest green and slate tones representing land, topography, and solid craftsmanship.
- **Zero legal jargon barriers**: Clear, transparent explanations of procedures, realistic turnaround timelines, and upfront price ranges.
- **Immediate actionability**: Direct one-tap phone calls and quick inquiry forms with minimal friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance (≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3.0:1 for large text).
- Responsive, mobile-first design with touch targets ≥44px and safe-area padding.
- Balanced line lengths (≤75ch) and minimum font sizes of 12px for micro-copy and tags.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations and interactive states.
